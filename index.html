Our Story

It began with a question.

A simple, almost uncomfortable one. How can an industry that revolves around care, emotion, and human connection still operate so blindly?

We had spent years inside luxury hospitality — in dining rooms, kitchens, back offices, and leadership meetings — witnessing both its brilliance and its fragility. We saw extraordinary creativity, artistry, and generosity of service. Yet we also saw something missing: clarity.

Restaurants were investing deeply in talent, design and ingredients — with almost no understanding of how guests truly felt.
